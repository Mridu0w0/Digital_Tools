## About The Project

DigiTools is a modern, fully responsive web application built for creators, professionals, and businesses who want to supercharge their digital workflow. Instead of juggling multiple subscriptions and platforms, DigiTools offers a unified hub to discover and manage premium AI tools, design assets, and productivity software.

The front-end is highly interactive, featuring a dynamic shopping cart, a seamless 3-step onboarding flow, and beautifully styled pricing tiers. It is designed to be lightning-fast, ensuring that users can find, select, and start using the tools they need in minutes, not hours.

<br />

## Top Features

Here are 3 amazing things this project can do:

- **Curated Premium Library**: Browse and access over 200+ high-quality digital products, including AI-powered tools, UI templates, and social media schedulers, all maintained in a dynamic React-powered catalog.
- **Flexible Tiered Pricing**: Choose the exact plan that fits your business needs with transparent Starter, Pro, and Enterprise tiers. The UI features dynamic hover states and highlights the most popular plans.
- **Dynamic Cart & State Management**: Seamlessly add digital products to your cart, toggle between your product dashboard and cart view, and manage your premium toolkit with a fast, interactive user interface.
